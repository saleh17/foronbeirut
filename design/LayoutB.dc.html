Why it works

One-handed on the bus, which is where this game gets played. The saj and the ingredients both sit inside the arc, so the two time-critical taps never need a grip change.

The tradeoff

Harder to scan — nothing lines up, so a new player has to learn the layout instead of reading it. Needs a mirrored left-handed mode, and the multi-finger batching from Shawarma Legend mostly stops working.
