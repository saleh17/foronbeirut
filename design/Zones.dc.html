Why this layout

Landscape gives two thumbs. The pipeline runs left to right across the counter — dough, topping, saj, serve — the same direction a baker actually works.

Everything you only read sits above the counter line, out of the way. Everything you touch sits below it, inside one thumb arc or the other.

Coins land on the counter where the customer stood, in the middle — reachable by either hand, but never free.

Watch for

The saj sits under the left thumb; serving under the right. At three slots the left hand gets busy — that is when the prep buffer upgrade starts to matter.
