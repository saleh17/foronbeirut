Why it works

Teaches itself: the screen reads in the same order as the recipe, so a new player never wonders what to touch next. Every station is legible at a glance during a rush.

The tradeoff

The saj — the one thing you must hit on time — is the furthest reach from a resting thumb. Two-handed play becomes mandatory once you run three slots.
